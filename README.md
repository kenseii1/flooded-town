# The Flooded Town

Row a small boat through a drowned town at night to the bell tower, and when the windows go dark, put out your lantern and hold still.

Play it free in your browser: https://kenseii1.github.io/flooded-town/

## Controls

Keyboard and mouse:

- W / S: row forward / back
- A / D: steer
- Space (hold): hold still, oars in
- F: lantern on / off
- E: light a lamp / hang the lantern
- R: back to the last checkpoint
- Mouse: look around (click the game first)
- Esc: controls panel

Phone or tablet (hold it sideways): on-screen buttons ROW, LEFT, RIGHT, STILL, F (lantern), E (use), R (restart) and ? (controls).
